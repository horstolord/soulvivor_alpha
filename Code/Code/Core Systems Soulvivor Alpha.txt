Core Systems Summary (Foundational Layer – Soulvivor--->Godyssey)

Design Philosophy:

• Systemic first  
• Same rules for player & enemies
• Power Fantasy, wield forces, break rules 
• Gameplay Pillars: Combat – Exploration – Character Development  
• Scales from rogue‑lite slice release → overworld → shared‑world/MMO  
• Optimized for modularity + future content updates, shared dynamics and recognizable franchise-features

Combat/Movement:

• Interact->Grab items, staggered enemies, (climb onto big foes?) 
• Walk, Run, Crouch->Slide(short invuln)
• Jump, Climb, Wallrun/jump,
• (Magical) Levitate, Teleport, Fly 
• Dodge (invuln frames + perfect window)  
• Block (windup + perfect block), Hit-based parries  
• Channel for mana, absorb energy, parry spells(timing)
• Light Combos, Charged Heavy, Kick, Throw
• Techniques, martial equivalent to spells 
• Later: Comprehensive Melee System(directional attacks, sophisticated parrying)

Magic:

Wield your minds power, influenced by elements. later: Divines(light/dark)
Split into 2 main aspects:

1. Contextual "Force Action"/Cantrip. Gather your Energy and use it depending on the circumstance,
   e.g. Sprint->Buff Mobility, Sheathe->Buff your weapon, Click->Attack with it. Interact
   with the world, depending on your mind's power. Can grab some energy instant (acuity)
   and channel for more power(will), from your pool(wisdom). Attune yourself to your force
   of choice via actions, gear, casting. These are in essence simple 2 rune effects. (force+method)

2. Rune Magic. Each spell effect consists of a combination of runes that must be invoked.
   Cast spells via catalyst or hand by weaving words in a ring.
   Power required, complexity * player ability determine cast speed, difficulty, spell level etc..
   On items, these grant mods and can be runewords, inferring additional effects.
   Engraved in the world, these activate effects/traps, move platforms etc.
   Come in 10 base levels, corresponding to the skill required. Some runes stay the same or start high.
   All manner of magical effects should be made available one way or another when possible

3. Later: Leave permanent effects in the world, e.g. teleporters, catalysts, influence biomes.
   Construct these effects with runes that work off seals/rings. Contraptions and crazy effects! 
   "Mindvision"-> Magic sense, lets you navigate, leads to content, solve puzzles, see energies
   Planes, like webway or the fire realm, leylines as objects that influence the zone your in.
   Attunement mechanic that rewards manipulation of game mechanics to your favor
   
   

Statistics:

Level - gained between runs, grant attribute points for player, modify base stats for monsters

Skills - measure of ability in a certain field from 1-100. Each skill governs a tree of talents.
         Use-based progression. These come in tiers. Players have a few slots for each.
       - Gain Attribute and skill points per level 
Later: Ascendancy(multi) classes depending on allocations

Attributes (Universal: Player + Monsters)
Six expandable core attributes split into Body and Mind.

BODY  
• Might – physical power, stagger, melee scaling  
• Swiftness – speed, timing, dodge quality, 
• Endurance – vitality, physical defense, resistance to body effects  

MIND  
• Will – mental strength, spell potency, force capacity  
• Acuity – mental speed, perception, insight layer (Witcher‑sense), 
• Wisdom – mental depth, rune complexity  

Consideration 3rd Domain/7th stat "Divinity"  

- fate, providence, luck

Attributes are raw data. They drive other stats.

Derived Stats
Generated from attributes + equipment + modifiers.

Includes:  
• Max Health / Stamina / Energy / Poise 
• Regen rates  
• PhysicalPower / SpellPower / Attunement  
• Resists
• frame / perfect window bonuses  
• Insight radius & perception level for the vision layer
• Movespeed
• Buffs
• Miscellaneous Modifiers

Runtime Stats (Mutable Resources)  
Always reflect moment‑to‑moment gameplay.

- Health/Stamina/Energy/Poise 
- divinity meter (limitbreak style power mode)
- specialty resource like rage 

Spent by movement, combat, dodge/block, casting, etc.

Damage Types & Resistances
Two categories:  
Physical: Slash / Stab / Smash  
Elemental: Fire / Air / Water / Earth  
	   Light/life/order - Dark/death/chaos

Universal damage pipeline:  
PhysicalResist → ElementalResist → (level/tier delta?) → Block/Dodge → Final Damage  
Identical logic for players and monsters.

General Gameplay Loop:

- The player themselves is a soul first and foremost. To actually play, they will take control of a vessel that will die if they do. new corpses appear on the heap of heroes between rounds.
- trapped in the nexus, the player can do excursions into maps with different objectives. usually they are required to survive and beat an boss. then they can choose between a more difficult wave and boss up to 3 times. Later: Add additional extraction mechanics/endless mode
-between rounds, spend currencies acquired to gain attributes and better starting gear/vessels
